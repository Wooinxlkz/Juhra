# Data Deletion & Unsubscribe Requests

## Data deletion

Because Juhra currently stores almost nothing about you (see [Privacy Policy §3](PRIVACY_POLICY.md#3-what-we-do-collect) and the [Cookie Policy](COOKIE_POLICY.md)), most deletion requests are things you can already do yourself, instantly, without waiting on us:

- **Clear your local consent/preference data:** uninstall Juhra, or clear the app's local storage folder. This removes the one piece of data (`juhra-privacy-consent`) the app keeps.
- **Nothing else is held**, since there's no account system yet.

If in the future Juhra has accounts, purchase history, or any other personal data on file, you can request deletion of that data by emailing:

**karimsc01t@gmail.com**
Subject line: `Data Deletion Request`

Include:
1. The email address or account identifier associated with the data (once accounts exist)
2. What you'd like deleted (all data, or something specific)

We will confirm receipt within **5 business days** and complete verified deletion requests within **30 days**, except where we're legally required to retain certain records (e.g. purchase/tax records for a statutory retention period) — in that case we'll tell you what's retained, why, and for how long.

## Unsubscribe

Juhra does not currently send emails or newsletters of any kind — there is nothing to unsubscribe from yet. If and when any email communication (transactional receipts, product updates, marketing) is introduced:

- Every marketing email will include a one-click unsubscribe link in the footer.
- Unsubscribing from marketing email will **never** unsubscribe you from purely transactional messages you need (e.g. a purchase receipt) — but those two categories will always be clearly separated, and we will never use a required transactional email as a backdoor to also deliver marketing content you opted out of.
- You can also request removal directly by emailing **karimsc01t@gmail.com** with subject `Unsubscribe`.
