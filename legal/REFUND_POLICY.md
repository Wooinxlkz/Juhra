# Juhra Refund Policy

**Last updated:** [DATE OF FIRST PUBLIC RELEASE — fill in before publishing]

## Current status

Juhra does not currently sell anything. The app itself is free to download and use, and no in-app or in-game purchase functionality is enabled yet. **There is nothing to refund at this time.** This policy is published in advance so the terms are clear before any purchase functionality goes live — not written retroactively after a dispute.

## When purchases are introduced

Once in-game or in-app purchases are enabled, the following will apply:

### 1. No hidden fees
The price shown to you at the point of purchase is the full, final price. No additional mandatory fee, surcharge, or charge will be added at checkout that wasn't already disclosed on the screen where you agreed to buy. Optional fees (e.g. a payment processor's own currency-conversion fee, which is outside our control) will be disclosed as belonging to that third party, not presented as ours.

### 2. Digital goods, generally final
Because purchases are typically for digital content delivered immediately (in-game currency, items, cosmetics, etc.), sales are generally final once the content has been delivered to your account, **except** in the cases below.

### 3. You are entitled to a refund if:
- The purchase was made due to a **verifiable technical error** (e.g. you were charged twice for the same item, or charged but never received the item)
- The purchase was made by a **minor without parental/guardian authorization**, where required by applicable law
- You are legally entitled to a refund under the **mandatory consumer-protection law** of your country of residence (for example, statutory cooling-off/withdrawal periods that apply to digital content in some jurisdictions — these rights are not waived by this policy)
- The purchase was **unauthorized** (e.g. fraudulent use of your payment method), subject to standard fraud-verification steps

### 4. How to request a refund
Email **karimsc01t@gmail.com** with:
- The order/transaction ID (from your purchase confirmation or the payment processor's receipt)
- The date of purchase
- The reason for the request

We aim to respond within **5 business days**. Since purchases will be processed by a third-party payment platform, the mechanics of issuing the refund (and how long it takes to appear on your statement) depend on that platform and your payment method — we'll tell you what to expect once that processor is named in the [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY_POLICY.md).

### 5. What we won't do
- We won't make refunds artificially hard to request, hide the process behind dead ends, or require you to cancel a subscription (we don't have subscriptions) to get one.
- We won't charge a "restocking" or processing fee on a refund for digital goods.
- We won't silently change these terms after you've made a purchase in a way that reduces rights you already had.

## Platform purchases

If a purchase was made through a third-party platform or store rather than directly through Juhra, that platform's own refund policy applies, and refund requests should generally go through that platform first.
